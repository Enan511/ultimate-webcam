UWebcam 1.3.2 — Android companion

UWebcam.apk is the phone app. Copy this file to another Android phone and open it to install. Android 9 or later is required. Allow camera access when requested.

To connect another phone to this Windows studio:
1. Turn on Developer options and USB debugging on the phone.
2. Connect with a data-capable USB cable.
3. Unlock the phone and authorize this computer's debugging key.
4. Open UWebcam, then open Ultimate-Webcam.exe on the computer.

Automatic orientation is on by default. Place the phone upright or sideways; both previews and the virtual camera follow its position. When the phone lies flat, the last stable angle is kept. Turn Automatic orientation off to position the picture manually in the desktop studio.

Minimum delay uses independent 240p frames, with 480p fallback when 240p is unavailable. Fast 1080p uses hardware H.264. Available lenses and video modes depend on the phone. Both modes have been physically tested on the Redmi A3 and Nothing Phone (3a).

The desktop requires the complete Windows package, including its ADB files, drivers, and this Phone App folder. The phone APK itself is standalone.

Removing UWebcam from recent apps stops its camera service. The desktop Close setting chooses between stopping capture and exiting (default), or keeping the broadcast running in the tray. Tray Quit always stops capture.
